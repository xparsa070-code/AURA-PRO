🇬🇧 English

🎧 What is AURA?

AURA REAL-7 is an advanced web-based music player designed to combine the experience of a Music Player, Mixer, and Audio Processing System in one environment.

AURA is not focused only on playback. It is designed around audio control, processing, effects, spatial sound, and music management.

🎛️ Multi-Mode Equalizer

AURA features three EQ modes:

- 12-Band EQ
- 32-Band EQ
- 64-Band EQ

EQ presets are authored around the 32-band layout and automatically resampled when switching between 12, 32, and 64-band modes.

🎚️ Audio Processing & Effects

The AURA audio chain includes:

- EQ
- Preamp
- Bass
- Treble
- Vocal
- Presence
- Air
- Balance
- Distortion
- Delay / Echo
- Reverb
- Stereo Widener
- 8D Audio
- Compressor
- Limiter
- Soft Clipping

These processing stages are connected together as a continuous audio-processing chain.

🌌 Spatial & 8D Audio

AURA includes stereo spatial processing and 8D Audio using a "StereoPanner" with oscillator-driven movement.

💾 Processed Audio Export

AURA can render the processed audio chain offline and export the result as a WAV file.

The export pipeline can include EQ, Tone, Distortion, Delay, Reverb, Stereo Widener, 8D, Compressor, Limiter, and Soft Clipping.

🎤 Lyrics & Karaoke

AURA includes a Lyrics system and Karaoke mode, with lyric-line navigation and synchronization controls.
