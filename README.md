🎧 AURA Music Player

A powerful web-based music player built around an advanced audio-processing experience.

AURA REAL-7 is a feature-rich web music player designed to feel more like an audio workstation than a traditional music player.

It combines music playback, multi-mode equalization, audio effects, spatial processing, visualization, lyrics, playlists, radio, media support, and processed-audio export into one interface.

🇬🇧 English

What is AURA?

AURA REAL-7 is an advanced web-based music player focused on combining music playback, audio processing, effects, library management, visualization, lyrics, and media features in one interface.

Instead of being just another music player, AURA is designed to provide an audio-workstation-like experience directly in the browser.

🎛️ Multi-Mode Equalizer

AURA supports three EQ configurations:

- 12-Band EQ
- 32-Band EQ
- 64-Band EQ

EQ presets are authored around the 32-band layout and automatically resampled when switching between 12, 32, and 64-band modes.

🎚️ Audio Processing & Effects

The AURA processing chain includes:

- Equalizer
- Preamp
- Tone shaping
- Bass
- Treble
- Vocal
- Presence
- Air
- Balance
- Distortion
- Delay / Echo
- Reverb
- Stereo widening
- 8D audio
- Compressor
- Limiter
- Soft clipping

The processing stages are connected as a continuous audio chain before the final output.

🌌 Spatial & 8D Audio

AURA includes stereo spatial processing and 8D audio using a "StereoPanner" controlled by an oscillator-based movement system.

💾 Processed Audio Export

AURA can render the processed audio chain offline and export the result as a WAV file.

The export pipeline can include EQ, tone shaping, distortion, delay, reverb, stereo widening, 8D, compression, limiting, and soft clipping.

🎤 Lyrics & Karaoke

AURA includes lyrics rendering and a Karaoke mode with lyric navigation and synchronization controls.

📚 Music Library

The interface includes dedicated sections for:

- Library
- Albums
- Artists
- Favorites
- Playlists
- History
- Statistics
- Radio
- Media
- Karaoke

🚀 Project Vision

AURA is not intended to be just another web music player.

The long-term idea is to create an audio-focused environment where music playback, mixing, effects, visualization, and media management come together in one experience.
